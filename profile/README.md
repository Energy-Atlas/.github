EnergyAtlas integrates **urban-scale building energy simulation, geospatial data, measured energy data, and agentic AI workflows** in a shared interactive environment. It supports workflows including:

* urban building energy modeling and scenario simulation;
* building-level calibration and model configuration;
* spatial exploration of buildings, loads, and energy-system characteristics;
* agent-driven simulation, analysis, reflection, and iterative model improvement.

Developed at the [Environmental Systems Lab](https://es.aap.cornell.edu/), Cornell University, Ithaca, NY.
